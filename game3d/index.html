Night Siege3D

One gun. One light.
Hold the castle against the darkness.

Stay at your gun position. Rotate the searchlight and shoot down low-flying drones.

How to play

  • Defend the castle. You have three health. Each drone that gets through costs one; zero ends your run.
  • Sweep and shoot. The searchlight reveals drones but does no damage. Aim at their bodies. The radar shows targets even outside the beam.
  • Watch your bullet slots. Start with three shots in flight at once. A slot frees up when a shot hits or expires.
  • Finish each wave. All drones must be destroyed or reach the castle. Later waves bring more, faster and tougher enemies.
  • Earn and spend points. Scouts give 10, armored drones 20 and heavies 30 points. Clearing a wave adds 20 and repairs one health, up to three.
  • Resupply. Buy upgrades with your points, or save them. After four seconds, press Start next wave. Purchases are optional.
  • Take a break. Use the pause button to freeze play. Choose your difficulty below; points and upgrades reset for a new run.
